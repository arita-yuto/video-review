# Videos

List videos and delete the files of a video or of one revision.

---

## 1. Open

Open Administration → Videos.
Title, folder, last update and the number of revisions are listed.

Type part of a title or folder into the filter to narrow the list.
The arrow at the left of a row opens its revisions.

---

## 2. Delete a video

1. Press Delete at the right of the row.
2. Type `Delete` into the confirmation field (same capitalization).
3. Press Delete.

The files of every revision are deleted and the video leaves the list.

---

## 3. Delete one revision

1. Open the revisions with the arrow of the row.
2. Press Delete on the revision and confirm by typing `Delete` in the same way.

The newest remaining revision is the one the video shows from then on.

- Deleted files cannot be restored.
- When some files could not be removed, the screen says so. Files left in storage are removed by hand.
- Uploading a video with the same title again picks the earlier comments back up.
