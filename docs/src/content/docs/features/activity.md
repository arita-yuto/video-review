Recent comments, uploads and reactions across all videos are listed in one place, newest first.  
You can see what happened since you last looked without opening each video.

---

## 1. Open Activity

Press the bell button to the right of Video list.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/activity/activity-button.webp" width="171" alt="The bell button to the right of Video list" />

Note: the bell button is not shown when you are logged in as a guest.

## 2. Filter by type

Choose what to show with the tabs on the left.

- All: all activity
- Comments: comments
- Uploads: video uploads
- Reactions: reactions (👍️) to comments

<img src="/video-review/images/features/activity/activity-dialog.webp" width="700" alt="The Activity dialog" />

## 3. Check what is new

Entries added since you last closed Activity are marked New.  
The New line separates new entries from the ones you have already checked.  
Closing Activity marks everything up to that point as checked.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/activity/new-entries.webp" width="700" alt="Entries marked New and the New line" />

## 4. Open a video from an entry

- Pressing a comment or reaction entry opens the video at the comment's position.
- Pressing an upload entry opens the video at the uploaded revision.
