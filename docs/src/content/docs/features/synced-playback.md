Everyone who opens the same video can watch it together, with play, pause, seek and playback speed kept in step.  
Each browser plays the video from the server itself, so no single person's connection carries the load the way screen sharing does.

---

## 1. Start syncing

1. Open the video you want to watch together.
2. Press the Sync playback button (the people icon) below the player.
3. A Share link dialog appears. Copy the URL or show the QR code, and share it with your teammates.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/share-link.webp" width="450" alt="The sync link and its QR code" />

## 2. Join from the link

Open the link you received and press Join.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/join-dialog.webp" width="450" alt="The prompt asking whether to join synced playback" />

## 3. Watch together

When anyone in the session plays, pauses, seeks or changes the playback speed, every screen follows.  
The number on the Sync playback button is how many people have joined. Hover over it to see who they are.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/participants.webp" width="200" alt="The participant count and the list of participants" />

While syncing, a few things work differently:

- Loop playback and auto play of the next video are turned off.
- Writing a comment does not pause playback, and comments are not posted to chat such as Slack, so you can comment freely.

## 4. Present

Press the Present button next to the Sync playback button to become the presenter. Only the presenter can control playback.  
Press it again to stop presenting.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/present-button.webp" width="100" alt="The Present button, pressed" />

### Hand over the presenter role

Any other participant can press the Present button to ask the presenter to hand over.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/request-handover.webp" width="270" alt="The button that asks the presenter to hand over" />

The presenter sees a prompt. Pressing Hand over makes the participant who asked the new presenter.

<img src="/video-review/images/features/synced-playback/handover-dialog.webp" width="450" alt="The prompt shown when someone asks to take over" />

## 5. Leave

Press the Sync playback button again, close the browser, or open another video.
